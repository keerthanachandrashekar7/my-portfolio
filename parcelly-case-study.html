01 — THE OVERVIEW

One place for every parcel.

Apartment residents may receive deliveries while they are away, miss updates or need to coordinate with a building desk. Parcelly explores a more organized way to see what has arrived and decide what happens next.

The experience is designed around a simple journey: find a parcel, choose a delivery option, schedule a convenient time and follow its progress through to handover.

02 — THE CHALLENGE

Make parcel delivery feel clear, not complicated.

THE PROBLEM SPACE

Parcel updates can feel scattered.

Residents may have to check multiple messages or contact people at the building to understand whether a parcel has arrived and how to receive it.

THE DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

Bring the next step into focus.

Bring parcel status, delivery preferences, scheduling, tracking and verification into one consistent experience, with a clear next action at each stage.

04 — VISUAL DIRECTION

A focused interface with warm accents.

The app uses a dark foundation, high-contrast type and warm orange highlights to draw attention to important parcel information and primary actions. Related details are grouped into clear cards so residents can scan status and delivery options quickly.

Design around the next useful action.
